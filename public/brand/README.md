# The Leopard Mark marks

Source files, unchanged, from the Marketing folder on Google Drive (Logos / The Leopard Mark):

- `tlm-wordmark-navy.svg`, `tlm-wordmark-white.svg`: Wordmark folder, `[SOURCE] TLM_Wordmark_Navy.svg` and `[SOURCE] TLM_Wordmark_White.svg`. The three-line name, vector paths from the original artwork.
- `tlm-crest-navy.svg`, `tlm-crest-white.svg`: Crest SVG folder, `[SOURCE] TLM_Logo_Crest_Navy.svg` and `[SOURCE] TLM_Logo_Crest_White.svg`. The plain shield.

Rules (brand note of Oct 28, 2026): navy #243167 on light grounds, white on navy, no other colourway. No recolouring,
gradients, shadows, outlines or distortion; never re-set the name in live type. The app shell sets the crest and
then the wordmark top left on the ground colour, with clear space between and around them equal to the height of
the word "THE" (30 percent of the wordmark's height). The crest alone is the favicon (`src/app/icon.svg`, the same
file) and the mark for anything under 96 px.
